🎛️ Nova Lite 2 hardware tricks worth using:
  • 2-way trigger locks (the switches on the back of LT/RT): flip them to short-pull for Apex/Warzone/REDSEC — shooting registers faster, a real edge in gunfights. Flip to long-pull only for driving/racing throttle feel.
  • Hair-trigger mode: with the triggers in the long-pull position, press M + LT/RT to arm hair-triggers.
  • Back buttons (M1/M2): map your most-spammed action here — jump (so you can jump without leaving the aim stick — "claw"/bumper-jumper style), ping, or heal. Set them below and mirror it in the GameSir app.
  • Hall-effect sticks: no stick drift — you can safely run a small inner deadzone in-game for more precise aim.
